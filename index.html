Performance Dashboard

FY2026 · Performance Snapshot

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) show how the City is doing on the things that matter most. They transform complex data into clear, measurable results so residents and leaders understand what’s working and where improvements are needed. By tracking KPIs, the City uses data to make informed decisions, prioritize resources, and improve services.

KPIs show how strategic goals translate into real-world outcomes for the Durango community, allowing us to celebrate successes, identify challenges early, and stay focused on long-term priorities like sustainability, infrastructure, and community well-being.

Ultimately, KPIs help answer the question: Are we moving in the right direction?

System Definitions

The two ways the dashboard organizes the City's work.

Strategic Plan Goal View

Strategic Plan Goal
One of the six community outcomes adopted by City Council in the 2024 Strategic Plan.
Objective
A specific, measurable aim under a Goal that describes how the Goal will be achieved.
Key Service
A core City service aligned to an Objective. Its KPIs show progress on that Objective.

Work System View

Key Work System
A major operational system that groups related City services.
Key Service
A core service delivered to residents and stakeholders within a Work System.
Program
An operational activity or service delivery function within a Key Service.
KPI (Key Performance Indicator) A measure, with a target, that shows how a Key Service or Program is performing. The same KPIs appear in both views.

Progress Key

What each status color means, wherever it appears.

On Track
Performance is at or above target, or within an agreed tolerance (e.g. within 5%).
At Risk
Below target but recoverable — within a moderate gap (e.g. 5–15%) or trending toward target.
Off Track
Significantly below target (e.g. more than 15% off) or declining for 2+ consecutive periods.
No Data
No current measure has been recorded for this item.
Gauge
The share of Key Service KPIs meeting their target (On Track or At Risk). Shown for Goals, Objectives, Work Systems and Key Services. KPIs that don't have a result yet aren't counted. Programs keep their status color.
Stale
Not updated in a quarter (3 months) or more — treat the badge as unverified until refreshed.
